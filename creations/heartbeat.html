Heartbeat

Canvas · JavaScriptInteractive

A 3D heart drawn by thousands of glowing particles in the warm golds and soft periwinkle of this site, floating above a platform of light. Move your cursor or finger across it to stir the particles. Tap it to release its energy and watch it rebuild itself.

How it works

  • 3D, not a flat icon. Particles sit on the front, back and rim of a heart-shaped volume, so it has real depth as it turns.
  • A real heartbeat. Two thumps and a rest, about 64 beats a minute, with waves of light climbing through it.
  • Alive to touch. Particles lean away from your cursor or finger; a tap releases the energy and the heart rebuilds itself.
  • Light on phones. Fewer particles on small devices, fewer still if frames slow down, and it sleeps when you scroll away.

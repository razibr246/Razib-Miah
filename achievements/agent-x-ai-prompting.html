Winner, Agent X AI Prompting Competition

16 November 2025AI · NetCom × Microsoft30th of the Top 50

I ranked 30th nationwide among the Top 50 prize winners of Agent X, an AI prompting competition presented by NetCom Learning Bangladesh and powered by Microsoft.

More than 50,000 participants from 47+ universities across Bangladesh took part.

The Top 50 were honoured at the Agent X prize-giving ceremony on Sunday, 16 November 2025, at the International Conference Hall of Daffodil International University, where I received my prize and certificate from NetCom Learning.

Finalist, YUNet AIgnite Buildathon 2026

21 November 2026AI · YUNet × OstadTop 20 of 425 participants

I made the Top 20 finals of Ostad Presents YUNet AIgnite Buildathon 2026, a national AI hackathon for consumer (B2C) solutions, out of 425 participants.

The buildathon was organised by YUNet (Youth Upskill Network), with Ostad as title partner and Presidency University as venue partner. Students from universities across the country built AI-powered web apps that solve everyday problems. After an online first round on Devpost, the Top 20 were invited to the Grand Finale.

At the Grand Finale on Saturday, 21 November 2026, at Presidency University in Dhaka, I pitched my project live and answered questions from a six-member jury, with judges from NRB Bank, the United Nations, Ostad, Presidency University and the software industry. Every finalist received a Certificate of Excellence.

How it worked

  1. Online round425 participants submitted AI-powered web apps on Devpost.
  2. Top 20KormoPath was selected for the finals.
  3. Grand FinaleLive pitch and Q&A with the jury, then the prize-giving.

My project: KormoPath

KormoPath is a Bangla-first set of 12 consumer tools for the journey from study to work and everyday life: opportunities, contracts and payouts, household budgets, health, farming and safety. The pitch was built around one idea: 12 consumer apps, one trust engine. There is no sign-up, personal data stays on the device, and money calculations follow fixed rules with their sources shown.

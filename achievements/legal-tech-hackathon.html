2nd Runner-up, Legal-Tech Hackathon 2026

26–27 September 2026Hackathon · UNDPTop 10 of 194 teams

With Team Law Debuggers, I made the Top 10 finals out of 194 teams and placed third at the Legal-Tech Hackathon 2026: Digital Legal Aid for Future, a national hackathon on making legal aid in Bangladesh faster, simpler and easier to reach.

The hackathon ran under the Accelerating Digital Legal Aid Services in Bangladesh (ADLASB) project. The European Union funded it, the Directorate of Bangladesh Legal Aid (DBLA) under the Law and Justice Division ran it, and UNDP Bangladesh gave technical support.

About 1,250 students took part. Over two days at Krishibid Institution Bangladesh in Dhaka, we built a working solution to a real national challenge and pitched it to a jury at the Grand Finale. We won a BDT 15,000 prize, trophies and certificates as 2nd Runner-up.

Our solution: DLAS

DLAS (Digital Legal Aid System) lets a citizen call a Bangla voice agent to report a legal problem and get a case ID. They can then track the case online while legal aid officers, panel lawyers and mediators handle it in their own portals.
